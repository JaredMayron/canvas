Time zones

Phoenix is 1 hour behind Dallas

Arizona does not observe daylight saving time, so in late November Phoenix (MST) runs 1 hour behind Dallas (CT). Landing at 9:12am MST is 10:12am in Dallas, and the 8:00pm Leno show is 9:00pm Dallas time.
