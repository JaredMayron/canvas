Know before you go

Warning

Bags slow down entry. Every bag goes through x-ray, which can add 30 minutes or more. Coolers, hard-sided bags, luggage, and any bag over 16 x 16 inches are not allowed and cannot be checked. Clutches up to 6 x 4 x 1.5 inches skip x-ray. Clear bags are not required.

  • Power banks are not on the arena's prohibited-items list. Charging stations are at Portals 2, 5, 15 and 19 (Plaza) and 38, 43, 54 and 64 (Mezzanine); bring your own cable.
  • Enterprise Center is cash-free at concessions and retail.
  • An empty plastic reusable water bottle (up to 2 liters) is allowed. Metal and glass bottles are not.
  • Stop in Play: wait for a stoppage before heading to your seats.
  • Rideshare zone: north end of 14th Street between Market St and Clark Ave.

Getting back to the hotel

The hotel is near Brentwood I-64 station, which is on the Blue Line. From Civic Center, take a westbound Blue Line train (toward Shrewsbury-Lansdowne I-44) to Brentwood I-64, then walk or grab a short ride to the hotel. Red Line trains do not go to Brentwood. If a Red Line comes first, you can ride it to Forest Park-DeBaliviere and switch to the Blue Line there. Rideshare from the 14th Street zone is the backup.
