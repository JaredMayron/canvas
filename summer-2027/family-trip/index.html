The Wyndham resorts

Each resort's quality tier, a short write-up, a photo, and its official Club Wyndham page.

WorldMark Windsor

Tier: no published tier (WorldMark does not rank its resorts). RCI: no award shown on its Wyndham page or RCI listing.

A relaxed WorldMark resort in Sonoma County wine country, about 10 minutes from the Santa Rosa (STS) airport. The 3-bedroom sleeps 8 with 2 baths, and the 3-bedroom Deluxe adds a third bath, so each of you gets your own room. Russian River kayak rentals in Healdsburg run May to October, and the Schulz Museum is close by in Santa Rosa.

Club WyndhamWorldMark Windsor →

Club Wyndham Vino Bello

Tier: standard Club Wyndham resort, not Presidential Reserve; not managed by Wyndham. RCI: no award found (listed on RCI as Shell Vacations Club @ Vino Bello).

A Tuscan-style Club Wyndham resort in Napa with pools and hot tubs. There is no 3-bedroom; the best fit is the 2-bedroom lockoff (1,329 sq ft, sleeps 8), so one of you would share a room or take the sofa bed. Napa Valley Paddle runs kayak tours on the river in nearby Carneros.

Club WyndhamClub Wyndham Vino Bello Resort →

WorldMark Leavenworth

Tier: no published tier (WorldMark does not rank its resorts). RCI: Gold Crown Resort Award, the top RCI rating.

Mountain-view buildings near the Bavarian village of Leavenworth, with the Cascades right behind. Units top out at 2 bedrooms (sleeps 6, about 1,000 to 1,050 sq ft), so one of you would share or take the sofa bed. Summer brings Wenatchee River tube floats and day hikes toward the Enchantments.

Club WyndhamWorldMark Leavenworth →

WorldMark Birch Bay

Tier: no published tier (WorldMark does not rank its resorts). RCI: Gold Crown Resort Award, the top RCI rating.

A WorldMark across from a calm, shallow bay near the Canadian border, about 2 hours north of Seattle. The 3-bedroom sleeps 8 in about 1,250 sq ft, and the 3-bedroom penthouse adds a hot tub on the deck. The bay is easy, warm-for-Washington water for kayaking, with Birch Bay State Park a short drive away.

Club WyndhamWorldMark Birch Bay →

Club Wyndham and WorldMark South Shore

Tier: standard Club Wyndham resort, not Presidential Reserve, though it offers upgraded Presidential and Deluxe suites. RCI: Gold Crown Resort Award, the top RCI rating.

A lodge-style resort in the pines at Zephyr Cove, on the Nevada side of Lake Tahoe, with an outdoor pool and two hot tubs. The 3-bedroom Deluxe (about 1,240 to 1,270 sq ft, sleeps 8) has a fireplace and a jetted tub. There is no lake view from the resort, but Nevada Beach is a 10-minute walk for kayaking and the Heavenly gondola is about 6 minutes away by car.

Club WyndhamClub Wyndham South Shore →

Club Wyndham and WorldMark Angels Camp

Tier: standard Club Wyndham resort, not Presidential Reserve. RCI: Gold Crown Resort Award, the top RCI rating.

A hillside resort on the 10th fairway of Greenhorn Creek golf course in California's Gold Country. The 3-bedroom (about 1,130 to 1,360 sq ft, sleeps 8) gives everyone a room. New Melones Lake, about 15 minutes away, has the warmest kayaking water of all the options, and show caves and Calaveras Big Trees are nearby.

Best pick if you don't drink

Things to do in late August that have nothing to do with wine.

Pick Lake Tahoe, at Club Wyndham or WorldMark South Shore. It has the most to do without a glass in hand: kayaking on the lake and out to Fannette Island in Emerald Bay, hikes from short waterfall walks to the Rubicon Trail, and the Heavenly gondola, coaster and zip line. It also has a 3BR you can book with either Club Wyndham points or WorldMark credits. Angels Camp comes second, with caves, giant sequoias and a 3BR of its own. Napa is the weakest fit because wine is the main draw there, but balloon rides, Calistoga mud baths and the Schulz Museum near Windsor don't need it.

Best value: Santa Rosa (STS) on Avios saves about 35,000 points over either portal, and SFO on United or SEA on Flying Blue are the next best if award seats open. How the portals work: Amex Travel books flights at 1¢ per point on the Platinum; the 35% points back is a Business Platinum perk only. Chase Travel pays 1¢ per point for the Sapphire Preferred plus Points Boost offers (up to 1.25x on economy, often United, sometimes Southwest). Points earned before Oct 26, 2025 on a card opened before Jun 23, 2025 still get 1.25¢ through Oct 26, 2027, so they cover an August 2027 trip.

How the transfers work: Chase moves points 1:1 to Southwest, United, British Airways and Flying Blue. Amex moves points 1:1 to Delta, British Airways and Flying Blue, plus a fee of $0.0006 per point (up to $99) on Delta. Neither card moves points to Alaska Atmos, so book Alaska seats with Avios. JetBlue has no nonstops from DFW to any of these airports. United, Delta Main Cabin and Flying Blue awards include a seat pick. Southwest points estimated at 1.25 to 1.4¢, United at the typical 12,500 Saver each way, Delta at about 1.2¢ per mile.
